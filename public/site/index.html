How your visit works

One smooth circle of care

  1. 1Book online

    Choose a department, doctor and time. You get a reference code instantly.

  2. 2Check in

    Show your reference at reception and receive a queue token.

  3. 3Nurse triage

    Your vital signs are taken and shared with your doctor.

  4. 4Consultation

    Your doctor examines you and orders tests if needed.

  5. 5Laboratory

    Results go straight back to your doctor — no paper chasing.

  6. 6Pharmacy

    Your prescription is waiting at the pharmacy when you arrive.
